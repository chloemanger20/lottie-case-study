Task 3

The bug

Questions for Hazeldene

  • Does this happen when you click send Invoices in Found itself, or somewhere else such as email?
  • What do you see when it crashes? Do you see an error message or does something happen? A screenshot would be really helpful.
  • Does it happen every time you try to send an invoice or only sometimes?
  • Does a single invoice send okay or is it only on batch sends?
  • When did this start, could you provide a rough time?
  • Are all four branches seeing this or just one?
  • What are you using - a computer or tablet and which browser?

Checking simultaneously

  • Any matching reports from other partners in the last few days?
  • Try to reproduce the error in a test account with both a single and a bulk batch of invoices
  • Any similar reports from other partners in Pylon
  • Check the partner's account for anything unusual: recent changes, invoice batch size, data that might not be saving properly
  • Check with Engineering whether anything was deployed over the weekend, since the timing lines up

Task 4

Stop it happening again

How do I add a new staff member to Found?

Also answers: adding a user, new starter, setting up a login, colleague needs access, someone left and I need to give their replacement access

Adding someone new to Found:

  1. Log in to Found and go to Settings > Team
  2. Click Add New User.
  3. Enter their name and their work email (each person needs their own login, even on a shared computer).
  4. Choose their role/permission level (this controls what they’re able to see and do in Found).
  5. Click Save. Shortly after they'll get an email to set their own password.

When someone leaves

  1. Go to Settings > Team.
  2. Find their name and select Remove Access.
  3. Their past work (enquiries, notes) remains auditable after they leave.

Can't see the Team page?

Adding users is usually restricted to admin accounts. Check with your manager, or contact us at support@lottie.org and we’ll point you in the right direction!

Tip: it's worth having at least two admins per home, so access doesn't get stuck when one person leaves.
